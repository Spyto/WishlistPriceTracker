Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Wishlist Price Tracker iOS app and the services directly connected to its operation.

1. Introduction

Wishlist Price Tracker is an iOS app that lets users add a publicly accessible wishlist, periodically track the prices of its products, and receive a notification when a product reaches the target price set by the user.

The app is designed according to data minimization principles. Only information reasonably necessary to provide, protect, and improve the service is therefore processed.

Wishlist Price Tracker:

  • does not require signing in to an Amazon account;
  • does not request users' Amazon credentials;
  • does not sell personal data;
  • does not use data to create advertising profiles;
  • does not track users across apps and websites owned by different companies for advertising purposes;
  • does not integrate third-party advertising systems;
  • does not use intrusive analytics tools intended to profile users.

3. Information processed

Depending on the features used, Wishlist Price Tracker may process the categories of information described below.

3.1 Wishlist link

When a user adds a wishlist, the app processes its public URL to identify the list and retrieve information about publicly visible products.

No username, password, authentication cookies, or other Amazon account credentials are requested or collected.

3.2 Product information

To provide the tracking service, publicly available product information may be processed, such as:

  • product name or title;
  • product identifier;
  • link to the product page;
  • public product image;
  • observed price;
  • product availability, when visible;
  • date and time of the latest check;
  • target price set by the user.

3.3 Notification data

When a user authorizes push notifications, a technical device identifier, commonly called a device token, may be processed. This identifier is used solely to deliver notifications related to the app's operation.

The device token does not directly contain the user's name, email address, or phone number.

3.4 Technical and diagnostic information

The systems providing the service may automatically generate technical information needed for security, error diagnosis, and correct request handling, such as:

  • date and time of operations;
  • request outcomes;
  • error codes or technical error descriptions;
  • app version;
  • technical information about the operating system;
  • IP address, when automatically recorded by network infrastructure or security systems;
  • pseudonymous technical identifiers required to protect and operate the service.

This information is not used for advertising profiling or to track users on third-party services.

3.5 Support communications

When a user voluntarily contacts support, the email address used, message content, any attachments, and technical information provided to resolve the reported issue may be processed.

3.6 Data that is not requested

To use the app's main features, users are not required to provide:

  • Amazon credentials;
  • payment details;
  • precise location;
  • address book or contacts;
  • personal photographs;
  • health data;
  • biometric information;
  • advertising identifiers.

4. Purposes of processing

The information described in this Privacy Policy may be processed for the following purposes:

  1. receive the public link of the wishlist added by the user;
  2. identify and display publicly visible wishlist products in the app;
  3. store the selected target price;
  4. periodically check product prices and availability;
  5. compare the observed price with the user's chosen target;
  6. send a notification when the conditions configured by the user are met;
  7. correctly synchronize the app with the backend service;
  8. prevent abuse, unauthorized access, and abnormal use of the service;
  9. identify and resolve malfunctions;
  10. provide support following a user's request;
  11. comply with legal obligations or lawful requests from authorities.

5.3 Legitimate interest

Some technical information may be processed on the basis of a legitimate interest in keeping the service secure, stable, efficient, and protected against abuse, while respecting users' rights and freedoms.

5.4 Legal obligations

6. Use of public wishlists

Wishlist Price Tracker operates using a link to a wishlist made publicly accessible by its owner.

The app accesses only information publicly visible through that link. It does not attempt to access private account areas or ask users to share login credentials.

Before entering a link, users must ensure:

  • that they have the right to use the wishlist;
  • that the wishlist has the intended visibility settings;
  • that the link does not expose personal information they do not wish to make accessible;
  • that they do not add wishlists belonging to others without permission.

Wishlist privacy settings are managed on the platform from which the link originates and cannot be changed by Wishlist Price Tracker.

7. Push notifications

With the user's consent, the app may send notifications about tracked products, for example when:

  • a product reaches or falls below its target price;
  • a significant change related to the requested tracking is detected;
  • a technical issue preventing proper tracking needs to be communicated.

Wishlist Price Tracker does not use notifications to send behavioral advertising or promotions based on user profiling.

Users can disable notifications at any time by opening:

iPhone Settings → Notifications → Wishlist Price Tracker

Disabling notifications does not necessarily stop product tracking, but prevents the app from displaying alerts on the device.

8. Backend and technical providers

To provide periodic tracking even when the app is closed, Wishlist Price Tracker uses backend infrastructure hosted through Microsoft Azure services.

The infrastructure may be used to:

  • receive and validate requests sent by the app;
  • store the settings required for tracking;
  • perform periodic price checks;
  • compare prices against the configured targets;
  • manage notification delivery;
  • record technical errors and protect the service against abnormal access.

Notifications on Apple devices are delivered using the Apple Push Notification service, commonly known as APNs.

Depending on the service used and applicable law, technical providers act as data processors, independent controllers, or parties authorized to provide the required infrastructure.

The main technical providers may include:

  • Microsoft Corporation, for Microsoft Azure cloud services;
  • Apple Inc., for the operating system, app distribution through the App Store, and push notification delivery;
  • GitHub, Inc., for hosting the informational website through GitHub Pages.

Each provider processes information in accordance with its own contractual terms and privacy policies.
