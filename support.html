Before you start

Wishlist Price Tracker does not require access to your Amazon account. All you need to use the app is a valid public wishlist link.

Quick start guide

Start tracking your products in just a few steps.

Step 1

Copy the public link

Open the wishlist you want to track and copy its public link using the sharing feature.

Step 2

Add it to the app

Open Wishlist Price Tracker, select the option to add a wishlist, and paste the link.

Step 3

Choose your target

Set your desired price for each product you want to track.

Step 4

Enable notifications

Allow notifications when prompted to receive alerts on your iPhone.

How to add a wishlist

For the app to read the products, the wishlist must be accessible through a public link.

1. Open the wishlist

Open the wishlist in your browser or in the app of the service where it was created.

2. Check visibility

Make sure the wishlist is not set to private. Wishlist Price Tracker cannot read a private list.

3. Use the Share option

Select the sharing option and copy the full wishlist link.

4. Paste the link into the app

In Wishlist Price Tracker, select the button to add a new wishlist, paste the link, and confirm.

5. Wait for it to load

The app will analyze the link and try to retrieve the visible products. The time required may vary depending on the number of products and your connection.

Important

Do not enter usernames, passwords, cookies, or other credentials. Wishlist Price Tracker only requires the public link.

How to receive notifications

Notifications let you know when a product reaches your chosen target price.

When you first open the app

When the app asks for permission, select Allow.

If you denied permission

You can manually enable notifications again on your iPhone:

Settings → Notifications → Wishlist Price Tracker

Enable Allow Notifications.

Recommended settings

  • Allow Notifications;
  • Lock Screen;
  • Notification Center;
  • Banner;
  • Sounds, if desired;
  • Badges, if available.

If you use a Focus mode, make sure the app's notifications are not silenced.

Managing wishlists and products

Setting a target price

Open the product you want to track, enter your desired price, and save the change.

The target should represent the maximum price you are willing to pay for the product.

Modificare il target

You can change the target price at any time from the product detail screen.

Stopping tracking

To stop tracking a product, disable it or remove it from the list, depending on the options available in your installed app version.

Removing a wishlist

From the wishlists screen, select the list you want to delete and use the removal option.

Removing a wishlist stops tracking its associated products.

Opening the original product page

When available, you can use the link in the product details to open the original page in your browser or the corresponding app.

Common issues

The wishlist is not recognized

Check that:

  • the link is complete;
  • the wishlist is public;
  • the link has not expired and is not broken;
  • the link also opens the wishlist correctly in a private browser window;
  • your device is connected to the Internet.

If the issue persists, try generating the sharing link again.

Not all products are found

Some products may not be imported when:

  • they are no longer available;
  • they do not display a public price;
  • they have variants that have not been selected;
  • the product page is temporarily unavailable;
  • the external service has changed the wishlist structure.

The displayed price does not match

The price may change quickly or depend on:

  • the selected seller;
  • the product variant;
  • availability;
  • coupons or promotions applied only at checkout;
  • terms available only to certain customers;
  • a delay between the automatic check and opening the page.

Before purchasing, always check the final price directly on the product page.

I am not receiving notifications

Make sure that:

  • notifications are allowed in your iPhone settings;
  • the product is still being tracked;
  • the target price has been saved;
  • the observed price has actually reached the target;
  • a Focus mode is not silencing alerts;
  • your device has an Internet connection;
  • the app is updated to the latest version.

The app shows an older price

Prices are checked periodically and not necessarily in real time. There may therefore be a short delay between a change on the original website and the next update in the app.

A product is no longer available

If a product is removed, made private, or no longer available to buy, the app may be unable to update its price.

In these cases, the product may be marked as unavailable or display the last known price.

Synchronization is not updating

Try the following steps in order:

  1. check your Internet connection;
  2. close and reopen the app;
  3. wait a few minutes and try again;
  4. check for updates on the App Store;
  5. restart your iPhone;
  6. contact support and include the error message displayed.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to enter my Amazon account credentials?

No. Wishlist Price Tracker does not ask for your username, password, or other login details. All you need is the public wishlist link.

Does the wishlist need to be public?

Yes. The app must be able to access the information publicly visible through the link. Private wishlists cannot be imported.

Are prices updated in real time?

No. Prices are checked periodically. There may therefore be a delay between a price change and the next update in the app.

Can I choose my target price?

Yes. You can set a target price for each product and change it whenever you like.

Will I receive a notification for every price change?

The main goal is to notify you when the price reaches your chosen target, avoiding unnecessary notifications for every small change.

Can I use the app without allowing notifications?

Yes, but you will not receive alerts on your device. You can still open the app to check the available information.

Does the app buy products automatically?

No. Wishlist Price Tracker does not make purchases, handle payments, or complete orders. Purchases are always made by the user on the original website.

Does the target price guarantee the final price?

No. The final price must always be checked on the product page. Shipping costs, promotions, variants, coupons, and different sellers can change the final amount.

Is Wishlist Price Tracker affiliated with Amazon?

No. Wishlist Price Tracker is an independent app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Amazon.

Is my data sold?

No. The app does not sell personal data or use information for advertising profiling.

For more details, see the Privacy Policy .

How can I report an error?

Email support with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a detailed description of the issue.

How to send a helpful report

To help us quickly identify the issue, include the following in your request:

  • a clear description of what you were doing;
  • the error message displayed;
  • your iPhone model;
  • your iOS version;
  • your Wishlist Price Tracker version;
  • the approximate date and time of the issue;
  • a screenshot, when useful;
  • whether the issue happens consistently or only occasionally.

Protect your data

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment details, or other sensitive information. Support will never ask for your Amazon account credentials.

Contact support

If you have not found a solution, you can send a request by email.
